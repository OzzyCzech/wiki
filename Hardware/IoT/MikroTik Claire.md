**CLAIRÉ** is MikroTik's indoor air quality monitor. It shows measurements on a **2-inch colour display** and connects to compatible smart-home systems through **Matter over Thread**.

## Measurements and hardware

| Feature | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Air quality | CO₂, VOC, PM2.5 and PM10 |
| Room conditions | Temperature and humidity |
| Display | Selectable views and colour-coded warnings |
| Power | USB power; maximum consumption listed as 1 W |
| Protection | IP20 |
| Product code | IAQM-THC2PV-W |
| Suggested price | US$99, listed on 2026-10-03 |

## Smart-home use

The display works without a smart-home setup. With a compatible Matter over Thread system, readings can trigger ventilation or notifications and reduce ventilation when conditions improve.

MikroTik describes a local setup using **Home Assistant and hAP be³ Media**, keeping measurements, history and automations within the home network. USB powerbanks and MikroTik GPOE-USB are also mentioned as power options.

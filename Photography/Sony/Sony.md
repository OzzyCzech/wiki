### Sony A7R VI

The sixth-generation high-resolution Alpha combines a 66.8 MP fully stacked sensor with the BIONZ XR2 processor. It shoots up to 30 fps with AF/AE tracking, offers stabilization rated up to 8.5 stops at the centre, and records uncropped 4K/60p. Sony launched it in May 2026 at an official Czech price of **128,990 Kč**.

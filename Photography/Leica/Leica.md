Quick reference for notable current **Leica** digital cameras. The SL system uses the shared **L-Mount**; fixed-lens Q models are listed on the compact-camera page.

## SL System

| Camera | Sensor | MP | Year | Price |
|--------|--------|----|------|-------|
| [Leica SL3-P](https://leica-camera.com/en-int/photography/cameras/sl/sl3-p-black) | Full Frame (BSI) | 44 | 2026 | 144&nbsp;990&nbsp;Kč |

The **SL3-P** is Leica's performance-oriented hybrid body. Its new sensor supports up to 40 fps with autofocus, 176 MP multishot, and five-axis stabilization; video modes include 8.1K/30p Open Gate, 5.9K/60p, and 4K/120p. The body is IP54-rated and adds direct Camera-to-Cloud transfer through Frame.io plus Leica Content Credentials.
